Dose-Response Meta-Analysis

Two-stage linear dose-response synthesis (Greenland-Longnecker): each study's within-dose covariance is reconstructed from the cell counts, a GLS trend is fitted per study, and the slopes are pooled by random effects. Distinct from network dose-response.

Studies

Linear = log-RR per unit dose (τ² estimator applies). Spline = non-linear Harrell RCS basis, multivariate (mvmeta) REML pool — captures J/U-shaped exposures. GL covariance reconstructed per study by design type. Verified vs R dosresmeta.

Knot placement (Harrell's recommended percentiles, R type-7 quantiles of the pooled dose distribution): 3 knots → 10th, 50th, 90th; 4 knots → 5th, 35th, 65th, 95th. The fitted knot doses are echoed in the results header.

Two-stage dose-response MA via shared/dose-response.js (Greenland-Longnecker covariance reconstruction → per-study GLS → REML pool). Linear: per-study slope pooled on shared/ma-core.js, matches dosresmeta(method="reml") to ~1e-6 (dose-response-parity.spec.mjs). Restricted cubic spline (3/4 knots at Harrell's recommended percentiles — 3: 10/50/90th; 4: 5/35/65/95th — via R type-7 quantiles of the pooled dose distribution): per-study multivariate GLS of the spline coefficients, pooled by a pure-JS multivariate (mvmeta) REML with a full between-study Ψ — matches dosresmeta(logrr~rcs(dose,k)) to ~1e-5 on coefficients, vcov, Ψ and fitted log-RRs (dose-response-spline-parity.spec.mjs). References: Greenland & Longnecker 1992, Am J Epidemiol 135:1301; Orsini, Bellocco & Greenland 2006, Stata J 6:40; Harrell 2001 (RCS).
