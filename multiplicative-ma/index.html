UWLS — multiplicative heterogeneity

Random-effects pooling adds a between-study variance τ²; the multiplicative model inflates the within-study variances by a single factor φ = Q/(k−1). The point estimate is the fixed-effect one; the SE is the fixed-effect SE × √φ. This unrestricted weighted least squares (UWLS) estimator is recommended as the primary analysis for observational meta-analyses — where inverse-variance random-effects weights amplify standard-error manipulation by primary modellers — with random effects kept as a sensitivity analysis.

Studies

When to prefer which: for observational syntheses make UWLS (multiplicative) the primary estimate; for RCTs either is defensible — report both. φ ≈ 1 means the data are consistent with a common effect; φ ≫ 1 indicates over-dispersion (heterogeneity or small-study effects).
