# p-curve + p-uniform

A browser-only tool implementing Simonsohn's p-curve (2014) and van Assen's p-uniform (2015) for publication-bias-robust effect-size estimation from sets of statistically significant p-values. The app accepts p-values via paste or CSV upload (`study`, `p` columns), filters to those strictly significant (0 &lt; p &lt; 0.05), and runs the p-curve tests: right skew by Fisher's combined method (−2·Σ ln(p/0.05) ~ χ²(2k)) and by Stouffer's method on the full and half (p < .025) curves, and flatness against 33% power by Stouffer's method on the full and half curves (z, or non-central t with a common df; p-curve app 4.x). A p-uniform effect estimate (method P, δ in z-units) with its 95% CI and test of no effect is also computed by root-finding. The exported results still carry three legacy fields (`prop_low`, `z_flat`, `p_flat`) from an older binomial screen; they are not the displayed flatness tests. All computation is inline JavaScript; no server required. Results can be exported as JSON, CSV, or Markdown via the shared export module.
