Baujat plot

Horizontal: each study's squared Pearson residual from the REML model; vertical: its influence on the pooled estimate. Studies to the upper-right drive both. Matches metafor::baujat.

Radial (Galbraith) plot

Precision (1/SE) vs standardised effect (effect/SE). The slope of the through-origin line is the fixed-effect estimate; points outside the ±2 band are outliers. Matches metafor::radial.

Standard influence diagnostics for weighted linear meta-analysis. Influential (marked "Yes" and tinted): |DFFITS| > 3/√(k−1), Cook's D > median χ²(1), hat > 3/k, or |DFBETAS| > 1. Matches metafor::influence(); applied to a REML random-effects intercept-only model. Heterogeneity quantified by τ² and I².
