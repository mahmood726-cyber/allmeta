Living-evidence pool with time-varying RoB

Re-pools your meta-analysis at a sequence of snapshot dates, applying the RoB rating that was effective for each study AS-OF that date. Studies with a re-rated RoB (e.g. a "low" judgment that became "high" after better signal-quality methods) automatically use the appropriate weight at each snapshot. Surfaces drift in the pooled estimate as evidence accumulates and as RoB judgements evolve — the two main reasons a living systematic review's bottom line changes over time.

Studies

Time-varying RoB is the key innovation: the pool at 2024-01-01 will use a study's RoB AS RATED ON 2023-12-31 (or earlier). The pool at 2025-01-01 may use a different RoB for the SAME study because its rating was updated. This makes the pool's drift reflect both NEW studies AND updated judgements on existing ones — the two reasons a living SR's bottom line moves.
