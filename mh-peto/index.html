MH / Peto pooling

For rare events, inverse-variance pooling on log-OR is biased. Mantel-Haenszel (fixed-effect) and Peto one-step odds ratio avoid continuity correction and perform well when event rates are low (< 1%).

Cross-tool:
MH for OR: Σ(a·d/n) / Σ(b·c/n), with Robins-Breslow-Greenland variance. Peto OR: exp(Σ(O−E) / ΣV). Cochrane Handbook 10.4.4 recommends MH or Peto for rare events over DerSimonian-Laird; Peto requires roughly balanced arms.
