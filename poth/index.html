POTH — precision of treatment hierarchy

An NMA ranking ("X is ranked best, SUCRA 0.82") hides its own uncertainty. POTH (Wigle et al. 2025) collapses that uncertainty into one 0–1 number computed from the SUCRA / P-score values: 1 = a fully certain hierarchy, 0 = treatments indistinguishable. Report it next to any SUCRA ranking — a low POTH means the hierarchy is not informative and "ranked best" should not be claimed.

SUCRA / P-scores

Subsets: to get POTH for, say, the top 3 treatments, paste the SUCRAs recomputed within that subset (re-ranked among those treatments) — not a slice of the full-network SUCRAs.

Result

Enter SUCRA values to compute POTH.

POTH = S²(n) / S²max(n) = [12(n−1)/(n+1)] · (1/n) Σ (SUCRAᵢ − 0.5)², with S²max(n) = (n+1)/[12(n−1)]; range 0–1. Median ≈ 0.671 across 267 published networks. Reference: Wigle A, Béliveau A, Salanti G, Rücker G, Schwarzer G, Mavridis D, Nikolakopoulou A. Precision of Treatment Hierarchy: a metric for quantifying certainty in treatment hierarchies from network meta-analysis. Stat Med. 2025;44:e70176. doi:10.1002/sim.70176. Closed-form computation in the audited shared/poth.js (analytically verified; mirrors the CRAN poth package). Computed client-side.
