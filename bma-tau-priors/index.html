Bayesian Model Averaging across τ² priors

Small-k random-effects meta-analyses are notoriously sensitive to the choice of τ² prior. Rather than picking one, this app integrates the posterior for the pooled effect μ over a panel of priors (half-normal, half-Cauchy, inverse-gamma, uniform), weighting each by its marginal likelihood. Output: posterior μ̂ ± SE with calibrated 95 % credible interval, per-prior contributions, and the τ² posterior mode under each prior. Drop-in sensitivity layer over any existing pooler — use it to bound the influence of the prior choice.

Studies

When to use: k < 15, sparse outcomes, or when reviewers disagree on which τ² prior is "right". The averaged posterior automatically downweights priors that fit the data poorly (low marginal likelihood). Reference: Friede, Röver, Wandel & Neuenschwander 2017, Biom J.
