Fully Bayesian random-effects meta-analysis. Normal-normal hierarchical model: θ_i ~ N(μ, τ²), y_i ~ N(θ_i, s_i²). Priors: μ ~ N(0, 10⁶), τ ~ half-normal(0, 1). Real Gibbs sampler runs in the browser.

Gibbs sampler: alternating conditional draws on μ | θ, τ, y; τ² via Metropolis-Hastings with normal proposal; θ_i | μ, τ, y_i. Convergence diagnostics: R-hat (Gelman-Rubin split chains) and ESS (effective sample size via autocorrelation). Chains are reproducible from a seed (mulberry32 PRNG; independent per-chain seeds). R-parity: seeded summaries are bitwise-reproducible and the posterior for μ and τ matches R bayesmeta's exact (deterministic) posterior within Monte-Carlo error, with R-hat≈1 and healthy ESS (hub/shared/tests/bayesian-mcmc-parity.spec.mjs). For serious Bayesian meta-analysis use bayesmeta, RoBMA, or Stan directly.
