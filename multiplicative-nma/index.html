Multiplicative-heterogeneity NMA

The network generalisation of UWLS. Standard random-effects NMA adds a between-study variance τ²; the multiplicative model instead inflates every contrast variance by one factor φ = Q/(n−p). The relative-effect estimates equal the fixed-effect (common-effect) NMA estimates; each SE is the fixed-effect SE × √φ, with tn−p intervals. Fit it alongside additive random effects and prefer it when AIC favours it by ≥2 — and as the primary estimator for observational networks, where inverse-variance RE weights amplify small-study/standard-error effects.

Contrasts (two-arm trials)

Scope: contrast-based core for networks of two-arm trials (independent contrasts). Multi-arm trials induce shared-arm correlation this engine does not model — feed each multi-arm trial as a single chosen contrast, or use a full multi-arm engine. With two-arm contrasts the common-effect fit and Q match netmeta exactly.
