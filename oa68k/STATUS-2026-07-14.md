### Labelled extrapolation (NOT measured — for planning only)
Applying the batch's per-meta rates to the 67,771-row corpus (naïve linear, no

sensitive and will move once the `label→PMID→NCT` layer is wired in.

## Honesty boundary (carried from the prior art, not papered over)
1. **Detect is conservative** — structure + trial links + high-recall error
   *candidates*. It does **not** claim gold primary-outcome 2×2 extraction; table
   selection is the named wall. The E5=473 figure is dominated by false positives
   (dosing ratios, reference numbers) — this confirms agy's "a blind text detector"
   warning and is exactly why candidates are for **source adjudication**, not verdicts.
2. **NCT-link rate is a floor** — most OA metas cite trials by author-year; the direct
   accession rate (27 / 169) rises once `pico-map`'s link layer is attached.
3. **Pre-extract confidence = copy-fidelity**, not truth of the registry value.

## Standing-job plan (how it grinds the full 68k on pc1)
- **Unit of work:** `python run_batch.py --batch 2000` — advances 2,000 un-harvested
  metas, idempotent, resumes from fsync'd ledgers on any kill.
- **Cadence:** schedule hourly (or a few large runs/day). At ~60 metas/min the full
  corpus is ~19 h of continuous harvest → **converges in ~1 day continuous, or ~2–3
  weeks of unattended hourly batches** with polite rate-limiting.
- **Memory:** streaming; only the done-key set is held in RAM (a few MB). Raw XML
  (~8.5 GB) lives on pc1 disk; **only the distilled ledgers ship** (the Tier-1/Tier-2
  artefact) — the "ship distilled, not the source corpus" rule, enforced by `.gitignore`.
- **Next wiring (highest-leverage, not done this session):** (a) attach the
  `label→PMID→NCT` link layer to lift NCT coverage; (b) scope the E1/E5 detectors to
  table cells (kills the text-regex FPs); (c) fold the redundancy graph (a trial in N
  metas = N human extractions) once ≥few-thousand metas are parsed.
