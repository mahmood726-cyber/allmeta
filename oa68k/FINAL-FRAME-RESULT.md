## What the four frames establish about the corpus

1. **Yield does not scale with candidates.** 2,594 candidates produced 6 comparators; 802
   produced 12. The cardiology frame remains the most productive by a wide margin, because
   its topics sit at k = 4–5 *and* its trials carry acronyms and PMIDs.
2. **The k ≥ 4 population is exhausted.** After the third frame, the remaining 106 unframed
   topics had k distribution `{0:18, 1:19, 2:41, 3:28}` — none at k ≥ 4. That was measured
   and declared *before* the fourth frame ran, as the reason to expect little.
3. **The threshold is easiest at k = 4–6 and degrades in both directions** — 100% overlap
   demanded at k = 2, thirteen trials at k = 26. My frozen justification for `k ≥ 4` was
   right at the lower boundary and wrong in the tail, and it shipped as a finding rather
   than being quietly corrected.
4. **PROSPERO is the largest single filter on matched comparators**, excluding well-matched
   Cochrane reviews among others. Recorded, never adjusted.

## On 40

**We are at 20 of 40 — exactly half, and every corpus topic that can match has now been
framed.** All 155 topic objects have been assessed: 49 framed across the first three
frames, 65 in the fourth, 4 excluded on their own declarations (`DUPLICATE PAGE`,
`RETIRED`, two near-duplicates), and 37 hold k < 2, where the frozen rule can never match
by arithmetic.

⛔ **There is no untouched lever left.** Every remaining route to 40 requires reopening a
frozen criterion:

- reopening the join — which was chosen **against our own headline count** on a hand-read
  of three papers, and is the strongest evidence we have that nothing was tuned to a target;
- relaxing `≥2 AND ≥50%`;
- dropping PROSPERO.

⭐ **Any of those is legitimate if it is declared in advance as a deliberate change of
standard, published, and re-run on the new rule.** It is only cherry-picking if it happens
after seeing which threshold produces forty — **which is exactly the position we are now in,
so that declaration has to come from Mahmood, not from me choosing a number that works.**
