Umbrella overlap · Corrected Covered Area

In an umbrella review (a review of reviews), the same primary studies often appear in several of the included reviews — so naively pooling across them double-counts evidence. The Corrected Covered Area (CCA; Pieper et al. 2014) measures that overlap from the study×review citation matrix, with Pieper's grooves (slight / moderate / high / very high), a pairwise overlap matrix, and the most-shared studies. Caveats

CCA needs the FULL list of primary studies each review cites (matched on a consistent id), not just counts — a missed match understates overlap. CCA is for the whole umbrella; per-outcome overlap can differ. High overlap doesn't invalidate the umbrella — it means any quantitative pool across the reviews must use a non-overlapping subset (or a citation-matrix correction), and the narrative should note the shared evidence base.

CCA = (N − r) / (r·c − r) where N = total citations across reviews, r = unique primary studies, c = reviews (Pieper et al., J Clin Epidemiol 2014). Grooves: <5% slight, <10% moderate, <15% high, ≥15% very high. Method integrated from the UmbrellaEngine project. Everything runs on your device.
