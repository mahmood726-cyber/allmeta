Reverse-Bayes — Analysis of Credibility

Given a pooled effect and its 95% interval, how sharp a sceptic's prior centred on no effect would have to be to overturn the result (Matthews' critical prior), and whether the finding is intrinsically credible — significant even against a prior derived from itself (Held). A robustness companion to the E-value, spec-collapse and fragility tools.

Pooled effect

Enter the estimate on its natural scale (e.g. OR = 1.6, CI 1.15–2.23). For a ratio measure the log is taken automatically; the SE is recovered from the 95% interval as (log hi − log lo)/(2·1.96).

Method. Sceptical prior N(0, τs²) with τs² = zα²s⁴/(θ̂² − zα²s²), defined when |θ̂| > zαs (Matthews R, R Soc Open Sci 2018;5:171047). Intrinsic-credibility limit |z| ≥ √2·zα (two-sided p ≈ 0.0056 at α=0.05; Held L, R Soc Open Sci 2019;6:181534). Normal approximation on the analysis scale; all closed-form, fully offline.
