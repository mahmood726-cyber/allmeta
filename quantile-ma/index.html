Quantile meta-analysis · heterogeneous treatment effects

A single pooled effect hides WHO benefits. Quantile meta-analysis pools the quantile treatment effect (the treatment effect at each quantile of the outcome distribution, from per-trial quantile regression) across trials, and tests whether the QTE profile is flat with a multivariate Wald test — detecting heterogeneous treatment effects across the outcome distribution (e.g. larger benefit in more-severe patients). Random-effects per quantile + Wald χ². Free, offline.

Caveats

Stage 1 (the per-trial QTE) must come from quantile regression on each trial's data — not from the marginal effect. The Wald test here uses a diagonal cross-quantile covariance (an aggregate tool can't see the IPD correlation between quantiles); since adjacent quantiles are usually positively correlated this OVER-states the contrast variance, so the test is conservative — a significant non-flat result is robust, but a null doesn't prove a constant effect. A sloped profile signals effect modification by baseline severity; confirm with a pre-specified subgroup/interaction analysis.

Stage 2: DerSimonian-Laird random-effects pooling (audited shared/ma-core.js) of the QTE at each quantile; multivariate Wald test on adjacent-difference contrasts of the pooled QTE vector, χ²q−1 under H0 (flat profile). Cross-language validated against numpy/scipy. Method integrated from the IPD-QMA project. Everything runs on your device.
