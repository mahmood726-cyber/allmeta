Everything model (outcome × time × RoB)

Joint hierarchical model that decomposes each effect estimate yi,t,o into outcome mean μo, study random effect δi ~ N(0, τ²), time-period γt, and an optional RoB-driven shift. Variational EM converges in 50-200 iterations — browser-tractable, no MCMC needed. Returns per-outcome μ̂o, per-period γ̂t, per-study δ̂i (shrinkage), and the converged τ².
