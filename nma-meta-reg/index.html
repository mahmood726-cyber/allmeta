Network meta-regression (treatment × covariate)

Adjusts pairwise NMA pooled effects for a single study-level covariate, with a separate interaction slope γ_k per non-reference treatment. The full model is y_i = X_i β + Z_i γ + δ_i + ε_i where Z_i = X_i · (x_i − x̄). Centring at the mean of the covariate gives β̂ the interpretation of "treatment effect at the average study." Predictions at any user-supplied covariate value come with proper SEs that account for Cov(β̂, γ̂).

Contrast rows + study-level covariate

Use a covariate likely to MODIFY treatment effect (mean age, baseline risk, year, dose). The γ̂ tells you whether the effect changes with the covariate — and how much. Always inspect the prediction plot to see whether the effect modification is clinically meaningful across the observed covariate range.
