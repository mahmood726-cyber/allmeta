t/F: independent two-sample pooled-variance design only; derives Hedges' g and its SE, not the SE of an OR, RR or HR. F must have numerator df=1 and needs the group direction. CI/p: normal/Wald approximation on the analysis scale.

Alternative representations of one estimate (SMD scale)

These points are correlated representations of one input, not separate studies or independent evidence. OR-to-SMD uses Cox's distributional approximation; RR, HR and IRR are not directly mapped to an SMD. Intervals use the delta method where applicable.

E-value (sensitivity to unmeasured confounding)

VanderWeele & Ding (2017): the minimum risk-ratio strength an unmeasured confounder would need with both treatment and outcome to explain away the estimate. For observational evidence only.

References: Borenstein et al., Introduction to Meta-Analysis (2009); Hedges (1981); metafor::escalc(SMD, vtype="LS") and metafor::cmicalc(exact). Cox constant √3/π ≈ 0.5513 relates OR and a latent continuous SMD under distributional assumptions. There is no general HR/IRR-to-OR conversion. SE is on the log scale for ratios.
