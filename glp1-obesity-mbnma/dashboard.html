Surrogate validation (weight → CV)

Weight loss is NOT a validated CV surrogate (error-adj R² degenerate/undefined, I²_HR=0%). Class k=7; albiglutide −1% weight yet HR 0.78. Clinical: weight cannot substitute for hard-outcome trials.

Joint benefit-risk frontier

Frontier: mazdutide, retatrutide, tirzepatide, semaglutide-sc-weekly, semaglutide-oral; dominated: orforglipron. ~2.19 pp nausea per extra pp weight loss.
