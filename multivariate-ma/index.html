Multivariate (multiple-outcome) meta-analysis

Jointly pool two correlated outcomes measured in the same studies, using each study's within-study covariance. The two outcome means are estimated together with an unstructured between-study covariance (REML), borrowing strength across outcomes — the mvmeta / metafor::rma.mv model.

Results text

Multivariate meta-analysis via shared/multivariate-ma.js: yi ~ N(μ, Si + G), with known within-study covariance Si and an unstructured between-study covariance G estimated by REML (μ by GLS; G by Cholesky-parameterised Nelder-Mead, with the exact REML solution for two studies). Fitted as mvmeta::mvmeta(Y~1, S, method="reml", bscov="unstr") / metafor::rma.mv(yi, V, mods=~outcome-1, random=~outcome|study, struct="UN", method="REML"). What is tested: an independent validation (P18) compared this page with mvmeta 1.0.3 on 92 two-outcome configurations from 22 installed R datasets (iterative quantities within 1e-6 relative to max(1,|ref|); exact two-study solutions within 1e-9), with a metafor 5.2-1 rma.mv cross-check on Berkey 1998; the in-repo browser test multivariate-ma-parity.spec.mjs checks dat.berkey1998 μ, SE and τ² to 5 decimal places and ρ and the REML log-likelihood to 4. The comparison/sensitivity panel is checked against a stored R reference (mvmeta, refined to a vanishing analytic gradient; metafor rma and rma.mv) on the same 92 configurations × 19 within-study correlations: 54,612 checks, protocol in validate/README-plus.md; re-run them in the validation panel above. Reference: Berkey et al. 1998, Stat Med 17:2537; Jackson, Riley & White 2011.
