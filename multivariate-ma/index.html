Multivariate (multiple-outcome) meta-analysis

Jointly pool two correlated outcomes measured in the same studies, using each study's within-study covariance. The two outcome means are estimated together with an unstructured between-study covariance (REML), borrowing strength across outcomes — the mvmeta / metafor::rma.mv model.

Multivariate meta-analysis via shared/multivariate-ma.js: yi ~ N(μ, Si + G), with known within-study covariance Si and an unstructured between-study covariance G estimated by REML (μ by GLS; G by Cholesky-parameterised Nelder-Mead). Matches metafor::rma.mv(yi, V, mods=~outcome-1, random=~outcome|study, struct="UN", method="REML") on the Berkey 1998 periodontal data to ~1e-7 on μ, SE, the G matrix, the between-study correlation and the REML log-likelihood (multivariate-ma-parity.spec.mjs). Reference: Berkey et al. 1998, Stat Med 17:2537; Jackson, Riley & White 2011.
