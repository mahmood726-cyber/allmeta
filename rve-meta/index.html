Robust Variance Estimation (RVE)

Meta-regression for dependent effect sizes (several effects per study) using the Hedges-Tipton-Johnson CORR working model, CR2 bias-reduced standard errors and per-coefficient Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, as in R's robumeta. Inference can be unreliable with few degrees of freedom; check the df column and how results change with the working correlation ρ.

Data

Columns: cluster, yi, vi, then any numeric predictors (the intercept is added automatically). cluster = study or other grouping of dependent effects; yi = effect size; vi = its sampling variance (vi = SE²). Comma, semicolon or tab separated (paste from Excel works). An optional header row is recognised (e.g. study, yi, vi or study, yi, se; an se column is squared to give vi). Quote labels that contain the separator, e.g. "Smith, 2010". Missing values are not allowed.
Assumed correlation between effects in the same cluster; it affects the weights and τ². 0.8 is the robumeta default. CR2 standard errors protect against a wrong ρ only as the number of clusters grows; with few clusters, check that conclusions hold at other values (e.g. 0.2 and 0.5).
Comma-separated, one per predictor column after vi. Filled in automatically from a header row.

Subtracts each predictor's mean before fitting. Slopes, their SEs, df and τ² are unchanged; the intercept becomes the expected effect at the predictor means. Recommended when the condition number κ is large (e.g. an uncentred year).

Inputs changed since the last fit. Press Fit to update the results and exports.

When to use: when studies contribute more than one effect size (several outcomes, time points, sub-scales or comparisons) and the within-study covariances are unknown. RVE does not need the true covariance matrix. References: Hedges, Tipton & Johnson 2010 ↗ (opens in new tab); Tipton 2015 small-sample correction ↗ (opens in new tab); R package robumeta.

Refits at ρ = 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 and 1 (robumeta's sensitivity() grid) and adds df, confidence intervals and p-values, flagging any coefficient whose sign or 5% significance changes.

Engine: shared/rve.js: Hedges-Tipton-Johnson 2010 correlated-effects (CORR) working model; τ² from the CORR method-of-moments estimator; CR2 bias-reduced cluster-robust standard errors (Tipton 2015; Pustejovsky and Tipton 2018) and per-coefficient Satterthwaite degrees of freedom; Student-t confidence intervals and p-values. Reference implementation: robumeta::robu(modelweights = "CORR", small = TRUE), cross-checked with clubSandwich::coef_test(vcov = "CR2", test = "Satterthwaite"). Designs with no more clusters than coefficients, or with collinear predictors, are refused.

Conditioning: each fit reports κ₂(XᵀWX), the condition number of the weighted cross-product matrix at the fitted weights. When κ exceeds 9.0×10⁶ (κ·u > 10⁻⁹, u = 2⁻⁵³), the first-order rounding-error bound κ·u exceeds 10⁻⁹, so agreement to the ninth significant digit is no longer guaranteed, and the app offers to centre the predictors. robumeta itself gives no such warning. The hierarchical (HIER) working model is not implemented; use robumeta::robu(modelweights = "HIER") in R.
