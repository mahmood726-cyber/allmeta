Rare-events GLMM

Binomial-normal random-effects logistic model for rare-event meta-analysis. Avoids the +0.5 continuity correction that biases odds-ratio pooling toward the null when events are rare. Uses 10-point Hermite-Gauss quadrature to integrate the random-effects intercept + damped Newton-Raphson on (θ, log τ). Equivalent to metafor::rma.glmm(measure="OR", method="ML") for the conditional approximation.

Studies

When to use: any meta-analysis where some studies have 0 events in one or both arms. Standard inverse-variance pooling with +0.5 correction biases OR toward 1; the GLMM doesn't because it models the binomial likelihood directly. Reference: Stijnen T, Hamza TH, Ozdemir P 2010 (Stat Med 29:3046-3067).
