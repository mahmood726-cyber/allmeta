Copas selection model — sensitivity

Copas & Shi (2000) selection-model profile maximum-likelihood sensitivity analysis. The pooled effect is re-estimated as the assumed publication probability of the least-precise (largest-SE) study is swept down, with the most-precise study held near-always-published. Each point is a full Copas profile MLE over (effect, ρ, τ).

Interpretation: γ = 0 means no selection (observed = true). Larger γ attenuates effects more strongly. If the curve crosses zero as γ increases, the observed effect could be explained by selection; otherwise, it is robust.
Copas JB. What works?: Selectivity models and meta-analysis. J R Stat Soc Ser A 1999;162:95–109 (seminal selection-model paper); Copas JB, Shi JQ. Meta-analysis, funnel plots and sensitivity analysis. Biostatistics 2000;1:247–262 (extension). Implementation: the full Copas & Shi (2000) profile log-likelihood (a faithful port of metasens:::copas.loglik.without.beta + its analytic gradient, box-constrained MLE) is maximised at each point of a reproducible publication-probability path. R-parity: the profile MLE matches metasens::copas() to ≈1e-4 on effect, ρ (where identified) and τ at the shared (γ₀,γ₁) points (tests/copas-parity.spec.mjs); unadjusted FE matches metafor to 1e-6. Not reproduced: metasens’s single auto-selected TE.adjust additionally requires a bit-faithful port of R’s contourLines() slope/goodness-of-fit machinery; this app instead reports the entire profile-MLE sensitivity curve. Per advanced-stats.md, Copas selection models need k ≥ 15 for stable estimation — this app does not enforce that gate; treat output as illustrative if k < 15.
