Copas selection model — sensitivity

Sensitivity of the pooled effect under assumed selection mechanisms (Copas 2000). The selection parameter γ controls how strongly a study’s precision (1/SE) determines publication. Output: adjusted estimate across γ.

Interpretation: γ = 0 means no selection (observed = true). Larger γ attenuates effects more strongly. If the curve crosses zero as γ increases, the observed effect could be explained by selection; otherwise, it is robust.
Copas JB. What works?: Selectivity models and meta-analysis. J R Stat Soc Ser A 1999;162:95–109 (seminal selection-model paper); Copas JB, Shi JQ. Meta-analysis, funnel plots and sensitivity analysis. Biostatistics 2000;1:247–262 (extension). Simplified implementation: per-study selection probability p(s) = Φ(γ₀ + γ₁/s), back-weighted inverse-variance re-pooling. Full Copas MLE in metasens::copas. Per advanced-stats.md, Copas selection models need k ≥ 15 for stable estimation — this app does not enforce that gate; treat output as illustrative if k < 15. R-parity: unadjusted FE pooling matches metasens::copas(metagen(…)) te_fe at tol=1e-4; adjusted direction (attenuation) and slope sign verified (see tests/fixtures/copas-tiny.R).
