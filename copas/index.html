Copas selection model — sensitivity

Copas & Shi (2000) selection-model profile maximum-likelihood sensitivity analysis. The pooled effect is re-estimated as the assumed publication probability of the least-precise (largest-SE) study is swept down, with the most-precise study held near-always-published. Each point is a full Copas profile MLE over (effect, ρ, τ).

Interpretation: γ = 0 means no selection (observed = true). Larger γ attenuates effects more strongly. If the curve crosses zero as γ increases, the observed effect could be explained by selection; otherwise, it is robust.
Copas JB. What works?: Selectivity models and meta-analysis. J R Stat Soc Ser A 1999;162:95–109 (seminal selection-model paper); Copas JB, Shi JQ. Meta-analysis, funnel plots and sensitivity analysis. Biostatistics 2000;1:247–262 (extension). Implementation: shared/copas-core.js ports the whole of metasens::copas() (metasens 1.5-3) — the γ grids, the ngrid×ngrid profile-MLE contour surface, R’s own contourLines() and pretty() machinery, the approximate orthogonal line with its per-contour regressions, the four-parameter residual-selection-bias fit (pval.rsb) and metasens’s TE.adjust selection rule. Where it differs: the box-constrained maximisation is a projected-gradient routine, not R’s L-BFGS-B; because optim’s default factr stops about 2×10-9 short in the objective, the two do not report bit-identical maximum-likelihood values. Stage 5 (the four-parameter residual-bias fit behind pval.rsb) differs the same way, and in a direction worth knowing: where this port converges further than metasens, pval.rsb comes out smaller, which can move the selected point down the orthogonal line or make the adjusted estimate be withheld where metasens reports one. What is checked, and where it falls short: the committed suite checks every deterministic stage against R to 1e-9 on one reference dataset (metadat::dat.hackshaw1998, k = 37) in three configurations, and the profile MLE against a separate metasens oracle on a second dataset (copas/tests/fixtures/copas-oracle.json, driven in a browser by hub/shared/tests/copas-parity.spec.mjs and in Node by copas/tests/copas-oracle.test.mjs). The validation archive extends this to 46 inputs — and reports that the objective attained here is worse than metasens’s at 84 of 42,498 comparable grid points, in 16 of 138 configurations. That was a pre-registered requirement and the archive records it as not met; see its DEVIATIONS.md. Neither optimiser dominates the other. What it costs: a fit is ngrid×ngrid profile maximum-likelihood maximisations on the main thread of this tab — 400 at the default. Measured under Node 24: about 3.7 s for the ten studies this page loads with, and about 7.9 s for dat.hackshaw1998 (k = 37); a browser is the same order. The elapsed time of each fit is reported above the results. Changing the y-axis range only redraws the chart; changing ngrid, the τ² method, sign.rsb or the CI level refits, and those controls are debounced. Small k: this app warns below k = 15 and treats 3 ≤ k < 15 output as illustrative rather than refusing it; k ≥ 15 is this project’s own operating threshold, not a published one.
