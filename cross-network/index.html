Cross-network synthesis (NMA + IPD + obs)

Combine RCT NMA effects, IPD trial-level summaries, and observational evidence under a unified bias model. Each evidence stream gets a design-specific bias offset δ and between-study bias variance σ², estimated by method of moments on the residuals relative to the RCT anchor. The synthesis effect uses inverse-variance weighting with effective variance v + τ²_RCT + σ²_design and bias-adjusted means y − δ_design.

Evidence

Each contrast is processed independently. The bias offsets δ_ipd, δ_obs are estimated per-contrast — observational data may be biased in different directions for different comparisons. References: Welton, Cooper, Ades, Lu, Sutton 2009 (Stat Med); Efthimiou et al. 2017 "GetReal in NMA" (Res Synth Methods 8:23-39).
