Review project

A whole systematic review as one offline, cryptographically-signed, tamper-evident file. This shell watches your live allmeta workspace: each pipeline stage shows its current status from the shared buses, opens its dedicated app pre-loaded, and folds its output into a SHA-256-chained, HMAC-signed bundle in one click. Re-opening verifies the chain end-to-end and pinpoints any stage that was altered — reproducible evidence synthesis in a single file, no server, no account.

Pipeline

Bundle

Sign with your HMAC key (set it once below); the signed bundle proves who produced it and that no stage was altered.

What's signed: the chain runs output → hash → next stage, so tampering with any stage breaks that stage's hash and every hash after it. The whole bundle is HMAC-SHA-256 signed (reusing the audited TruthCert signer); with no key set, the bundle still carries the tamper-evident chain but is unsigned (never a fake signature).
Provenance chain + HMAC via shared/review-bundle.js (SHA-256 chain) over the audited shared/ma-studies-v1.js TruthCert signer and shared/truthcert-export.js hash. Stage outputs are the JSON exports of the linked allmeta apps. Everything is client-side; the bundle never leaves your device.
