one offline, signed, tamper-evident systematic review — data flows stage → stage through your live workspace
✓Bundle & sign

Fold every captured stage into one SHA-256-chained, HMAC-signed file. The chain runs output → hash → next stage, so altering any stage breaks that stage's hash and every hash after it. Re-opening a bundle verifies the whole chain and pinpoints any altered stage.

What's signed: with no key set, the bundle still carries the tamper-evident chain but is unsigned (never a fake signature). Set your HMAC key once to sign.
Provenance chain + HMAC via shared/review-bundle.js over the audited shared/ma-studies-v1.js / shared/ma-pooled-v1.js workspace buses and shared/truthcert-export.js hash. Each stage's data is read live from the same buses its app writes — extraction's studies flow into synthesis, appraisal and the integrity screens automatically. Everything is client-side; the bundle never leaves your device.
