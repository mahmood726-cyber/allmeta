Freeman-Tukey: FT = ½(arcsin√(x/(n+1)) + arcsin√((x+1)/(n+1))), variance 1/(4n+2) (Miller 1978). Caveat (Schwarzer et al. Res Synth Methods 2019;10:476-483): FT back-transformed pooled CI can be nonsensical in some settings; prefer logit + continuity correction or GLMM (metafor::rma.glmm) for rare-event proportions. Logit: ln(p/(1-p)), variance 1/(x+0.5) + 1/(n-x+0.5) with Cochrane Handbook §10.4.4 / Sweeting 2004 correction at zeros (cn=n+1). Clopper-Pearson: qbeta(α/2, x, n-x+1). R-parity: logit+PM matches metafor::escalc(measure="PLO") + rma(method="PM") to tolerance 1e-6 (see tests/fixtures/prop-tiny.R).
