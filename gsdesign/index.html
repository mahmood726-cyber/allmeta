Group-Sequential Design

Exact efficacy (and optional beta-spending futility) boundaries for a K-look group-sequential trial, from the canonical joint distribution of the sequential Z-statistics via recursive Gauss-Legendre integration (Armitage-McPherson-Rowe). Matches gsDesign. Unlike the meta-analysis monitoring tools (TSA, sequential-MA), this is the exact boundary, not the closed-form approximation.

Design

Equally-spaced information fractions (i/K). Nominal p is the per-look two-sided/one-sided crossing p-value.

Method. Sequential Z with independent increments (Cov(Zi,Zj)=√(ti/tj)); boundaries solved by the recursive-density method (Armitage, McPherson & Rowe 1969) with 32-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature and bisection on each look's incremental spend. Spending: O'Brien-Fleming & Pocock (Lan & DeMets 1983), power (Lan-DeMets ρ), Hwang-Shih-DeCani γ (1990). Engine shared/gsdesign.js, verified vs gsDesign::gsDesign to ~1e-5. Fully offline.
