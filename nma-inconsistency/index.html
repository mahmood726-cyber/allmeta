NMA Inconsistency

Detect inconsistency between direct and indirect evidence in network meta-analysis. Performs node-splitting (Dias et al. 2010) on every comparison with a closed loop, and a global design-by-treatment test (Higgins et al. 2012).

Reading the plot: direct and indirect whiskers should overlap for a consistent network. Non-overlapping whiskers with |z| > 1.96 indicate a specific loop with inconsistency. The global design-by-treatment test is the omnibus check.
Node-splitting: Dias S et al. Stat Med 2010;29:932-944. Design-by-treatment inconsistency model: Higgins JPT, Jackson D, Barrett JK et al. Res Synth Methods 2012;3:98-110. R-parity: the fixed-effect engine matches netmeta(common=TRUE) + netsplit() + decomp.design() to 1e-4 on network estimates, per-comparison direct/indirect/diff/z/p, and the design-by-treatment Q/df/p (exact χ²; see hub/shared/tests/nma-inconsistency-parity.spec.mjs). Random-effects (2026-05-24): select PM or DL above to use shared τ² inflated weights (τ² from the consistency-model residuals) for both consistency and inconsistency models — addresses the prior FE-only caveat. Recommended k≥5 per design.
