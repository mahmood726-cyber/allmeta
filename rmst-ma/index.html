RMST Meta-Analysis

Pool restricted mean survival time (RMST) differences (treatment − control, at a common horizon τ*) across trials. RMST differences are on the natural time scale — no proportional-hazards assumption — and pool additively. Pair with the KM-reconstructor to obtain per-trial RMST from published curves.

Studies

Inverse-variance pooling on the RMST-difference scale via the audited ma-core; 95% prediction interval (tk−1, Cochrane v6.5). State τ* (the horizon) in the report — RMST is only comparable across trials at the same τ*.
