Screen · local citation screening

Screen titles and abstracts, then assess retrieved full texts. Review independently, reconcile disagreements, and keep a dated history with your project backup. Local by default; AI assistance is optional.

What to do on this page — decide which records to include

Read each record and decide whether it belongs in your review. This is a human judgement the tool cannot make for you.

  1. Import records (from Search or a file), or click Load example.
  2. For each card, read the abstract. Where a journal (PubMed) abstract exists it is shown in preference; records that only have a ClinicalTrials.gov brief summary are labelled as such — use the ClinicalTrials.gov / PubMed / DOI buttons to open the source and check it.
  3. Press i (include), e (exclude) or m (maybe); add a reason for excludes.
  4. In dual-reviewer mode, resolve any conflicts so each record has one agreed decision.
  5. When done, send the included records on to Extraction.

The tool does

Flags possible duplicates, an offline RCT model score and your search-term matches — these are hints.

You must check

Every include / exclude decision, and that the final included set is correct, before moving on.

Cold-start RCT classifier

The RCT model score is a ranking signal, not a calibrated probability or a decision about study design. The PubMed sample was approximately balanced between RCT and non-RCT publication types. Negatives were reviews, observational studies, case reports and comparative studies. Non-randomised interventional designs were absent as an explicit negative class; their coverage and performance are not established. Protocols, single-arm and quasi-randomised studies can score highly. This is an English-oriented model; non-English performance is not validated. Check random allocation and eligibility yourself.

Local ML ranking

Label a few records with i/e, then train an in-browser classifier (TF-IDF + Naive Bayes, with class-balanced weighting) to rank the rest by predicted relevance. Retrain after each small batch for continuous active learning. 100% local, no key, reproducible.

Not trained yet.

Features: title/abstract unigrams + MeSH/keywords. Blind mode trains only on your labels; unblinded mode can use both reviewers. Scores are not calibrated probabilities. Cross-validation describes the labelled subset, not unseen-record recall. No automatic stopping or exclusion.

    Conflicts to resolve (0)

    Local by default — with AI assist off, records never leave your device. The optional bring-your-own-key path is the only exception: it sends the title and abstract of each screened record to the AI provider you choose (which is why connect-src allowlists those three hosts), and your key is stored only in this browser. Dedup: exact DOI matches are removed automatically. Title similarity (trigram-Jaccard ≥0.85, or ≥0.72 with the same first author and year) produces possible duplicates for confirmation. Different nonempty DOIs never merge. Candidate pairs come from blocking + a sorted-neighbourhood window, to reduce pair comparisons; this upgrade does not claim measured 100k-record throughput; earliest-imported record wins. Agreement: unweighted Cohen's κ over include/exclude pairs only; maybe and missing ratings are omitted. SE and 95% CI use the Fleiss-Cohen-Everitt asymptotic method. Effective decision in dual mode: adjudication, agreement, conflict, or pending until both reviewers decide. PRISMA push writes the prisma-flow-v1 key. Relevance is a transparent term-frequency score over your own include/exclude terms — no cloud model, no black box.
