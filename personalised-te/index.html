Personalised TE synthesis (subgroup shrinkage)

Empirical Bayes shrinkage of subgroup-specific effect estimates. Each subgroup's pooled effect is pulled toward the overall mean by an amount inversely proportional to its precision; rare or noisy subgroups get heavy shrinkage. Personalised prediction: given a patient's subgroup label, return the EB posterior estimate (or fall back to the overall pooled effect when the subgroup wasn't observed).
