Benefit-risk MCDA · rank treatments under uncertainty

Combine multiple benefits and harms into a defensible ranking. Each criterion is mapped to a 0–1 partial value, weighted, and summed; then SMAA propagates each treatment's effect uncertainty (mean ± SE) by Monte Carlo to give rank-acceptability (P best), and EVPI quantifies the value of resolving the remaining decision uncertainty — i.e. whether more research is worth it. Standard ISPOR / Tervonen-SMAA framework, free and offline.

Caveats (read before deciding)

MCDA is a structured value judgement, not a fact. The weights encode whose preferences matter and should be elicited/varied, not guessed. The partial value functions are linear over the observed range (a simplification). SMAA propagates the statistical uncertainty in each effect (mean ± SE) but not structural/model uncertainty. EVPI is on the 0–1 value scale; multiply by the affected population and decision horizon for a population EVPI. Vary the weights and re-run before acting.

Method: linear partial value functions v(x)∈[0,1] (benefit: higher better; harm: lower better) over each criterion's range; total value = Σ wⱼ·vⱼ. SMAA (Tervonen & Lahdelma 2007) samples each treatment×criterion ~ N(mean, SE) and tallies the argmax to get rank-acceptability; EVPI = E[maxₜ Vₜ] − maxₜ E[Vₜ] from the same draws. Deterministic (seeded PRNG) so runs reproduce. Idea integrated from the glp1-obesity-mbnma benefit-risk arm; generalised to a standard MCDA + VOI workflow (linear value functions, statistical-uncertainty SMAA). Everything runs on your device.
