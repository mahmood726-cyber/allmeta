INSPECT-SR

INveStigating ProblEmatic Clinical Trials in Systematic Reviews (Wilkinson et al., BMJ 2026, with Cochrane): assess each RCT's trustworthiness across 21 checks in 4 domains, reach domain-level and overall judgements, then re-pool the meta-analysis excluding flagged trials to see whether the result survives. Orthogonal to risk-of-bias and GRADE — it asks whether the data can be trusted at all.

Robustness — does the pooled effect survive?

Random-effects (Paule-Mandel) pool of trials with an entered effect + SE, under the INSPECT-SR handling rule: serious-concern trials excluded; some-concern trials tested by sensitivity analysis. Trials without an overall judgement are counted only in the first row.

Not a prescriptive algorithm. INSPECT-SR guides reviewers to reach justified judgements; the suggested domain/overall ratings here are non-binding cues from the check responses — the reviewer decides. Handling per the tool: serious concerns → exclude; some concerns → sensitivity analysis; no concerns → standard analysis. Median assessment time ≈ 45 min/RCT in the developers' preprint user testing (not measured for this page).
21 checks across 4 domains (post-publication notices; conduct, governance and transparency; text and figures; results in the study), each answered Yes / No / Unclear / Not applicable, rolled up to domain and overall judgements of No / Some / Serious concerns. Reference: Wilkinson J, et al. INSPECT-SR tool for assessing trustworthiness of randomised controlled trials. BMJ 2026;394:e100611. doi:10.1136/bmj-2026-100611 (preprint: medRxiv doi:10.1101/2025.09.03.25334905). Check wording follows guidance document v1.1.2 (https://inspect-sr.com). Developed in partnership with Cochrane. The robustness re-pool uses shared/ma-core.js (Paule-Mandel random effects); the validation of this page's decision rules does not test the pooling — use "Check the re-pool in R" for that.
