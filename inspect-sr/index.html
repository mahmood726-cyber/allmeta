INSPECT-SR

INveStigating ProblEmatic Clinical Trials in Systematic Reviews (Wilkinson et al. 2025, with Cochrane): assess each RCT's trustworthiness across 21 checks in 4 domains, reach domain-level and overall judgements, then re-pool the meta-analysis excluding flagged trials to see whether the result survives. Orthogonal to risk-of-bias and GRADE — it asks whether the data can be trusted at all.

Not a prescriptive algorithm. INSPECT-SR guides reviewers to reach justified judgements; the suggested domain/overall ratings here are non-binding cues from the check responses — the reviewer decides. Handling per the tool: serious concerns → exclude; some concerns → sensitivity analysis; no concerns → standard analysis. Median assessment time ≈ 45 min/RCT.
21 checks across 4 domains (post-publication notices; conduct, governance & transparency; text & publication details; results in the study), each answered Yes / No / Unclear / Not applicable, rolled up to domain and overall judgements of No / Some / Serious concerns. Reference: Wilkinson J, et al. INSPECT-SR: a tool for assessing the trustworthiness of randomised controlled trials. medRxiv 2025. doi:10.1101/2025.09.03.25334905. Developed in partnership with Cochrane. The robustness re-pool uses the audited shared/ma-core.js (Paule-Mandel random effects). Data stays in your browser (localStorage).
