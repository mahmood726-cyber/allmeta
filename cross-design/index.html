Cross-design synthesis (RCT + observational)

Combine RCT and observational evidence with bias-adjusted methods rather than naive pooling. Three estimators side-by-side: (1) naive pool — anti-conservative reference, (2) power prior — observational studies enter at downweight α ∈ [0, 1], (3) Welton design-bias random-effects — RCTs anchor μ; observational studies estimate the bias δ_obs and its between-obs-study variance σ²_bias separately. Pick the estimator whose assumptions match your trust in the obs evidence.

Studies

When to use what: If RCT and observational estimates agree, all three methods give similar μ̂ — use the most efficient. If they disagree, the design-bias RE explicitly quantifies δ_obs (the size of the gap) and σ²_bias (how reliable the obs studies are amongst themselves); naive pooling buries both. Power prior is a middle ground when you trust obs studies partially.
