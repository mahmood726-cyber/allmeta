Subgroup between-group Q compares random-effects pooled estimates across groups (each group pooled with Paule-Mandel τ², per Borenstein 2009 §19); Q_bw = Σ w_g (μ_g − M)², df = G − 1, where w_g = 1/SE(μ_g)² and the centre M = Σ w_g μ_g / Σ w_g is the inverse-variance-weighted mean of the subgroup estimates (matches meta::metagen Q.b.random). I² of differences = max(0, 100·(Q − df)/Q).
