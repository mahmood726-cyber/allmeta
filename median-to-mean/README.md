# Median-to-Mean Converter

Converts median, IQR, and range summaries to estimated means and SDs (Wan 2014 mean and SD, Luo 2018 mean, Shi 2020 SD for the five-number summary; for the IQR summary the Shi row repeats Wan's SD) for inclusion in meta-analysis.

Part of the [allmeta](https://github.com/mahmood726-cyber/allmeta) collection.

Live: https://mahmood726-cyber.github.io/allmeta/median-to-mean/
