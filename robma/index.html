Robust Bayesian Meta-Analysis

Bayesian model-averaging over the presence/absence of an effect and of heterogeneity, with inclusion Bayes factors and a model-averaged estimate — computed by deterministic quadrature (no MCMC). This is the effect×heterogeneity core of RoBMA; the full publication-bias ensemble is one click away in R.

Studies

Priors: effect μ ~ Normal(0, 1), heterogeneity τ ~ Half-Normal(0, 1); four equally-weighted models (effect × heterogeneity). Marginal likelihoods by Gauss/adaptive quadrature, verified vs R integrate().

Robust Bayesian evidence

Modelposterior prob.marginal lik.

Inclusion Bayes factor > 3 = moderate, > 10 = strong evidence (Jeffreys). The model-averaged estimate shrinks the effect toward 0 by the posterior probability that the effect is absent.

Robust Bayesian model-averaging via shared/robma.js: marginal likelihoods of the four effect×heterogeneity models by adaptive quadrature (verified vs R integrate() to ~1e-6 — robma-parity.spec.mjs). This is RoBMA's effect/heterogeneity sub-ensemble; the full RoBMA (Maier, Bartoš & Wagenmakers 2023) adds publication-bias weight-function & PET-PEESE models via MCMC + bridge sampling — use the downloadable R script with the RoBMA package for that.
