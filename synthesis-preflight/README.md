# Synthesis — author-side research verification v1.2

Public landing page for the comprehensive Synthesis author-verification **prompt-based workflow**, with source-led checking, deterministic arithmetic examples, mandatory benign-explanation testing and explicit abstentions. This is **not** a newly trained model, automated manuscript verifier or validated correctness certificate.

- [Live site (only after merge and Pages deployment)](https://mahmood726-cyber.github.io/allmeta/synthesis-preflight/)
- [Full prompt text](prompt-v1.2.txt)
- [Source webpage](index.html)

## Privacy

The webpage is static, does not upload manuscript content and makes no model or telemetry calls. The two example calculators execute locally, do not connect to a network and are demonstrations only. Users must confirm permissions before supplying confidential or sensitive research to external AI tools.

## Limits and validation

The historical Synthesis issue correspondence is a development corpus, not an unbiased validation cohort. No prospective sensitivity, precision, false-positive rate or editor adoption is claimed. A human scientific review is essential for material issues. This author-facing prompt does not replace the complete internal Synthesis governance system or protected Meta-Harness validation.
