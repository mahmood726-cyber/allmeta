Multi-outcome NMA (Achana 2014)

Joint network meta-analysis across multiple correlated outcomes, with the multi-arm τ²/2 off-diagonal correction layered on the K-outcome between-study covariance Σ_RE^outcomes. Implements Achana et al. 2014 §3.2 (Res Synth Methods 5:35-49). When outcomes are correlated, joint pooling borrows strength across them — studies that report only a subset of outcomes still contribute to the joint network estimates via ρ_between.

Studies

Multi-arm trials with 3+ arms: contribute 2 contrast rows per outcome (the multi-arm correction is automatic — same studyId across rows triggers τ²/2 off-diagonals). The Σ_RE^outcomes estimate uses DL τ² per outcome + sample-correlation of per-row residuals — fast and good for typical clinical data. Use the K-variate REML in multi-outcome-ma/ for slow-but-exact estimation on small networks.
