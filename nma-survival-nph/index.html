Non-PH Survival Network Meta-Analysis

When the hazard ratio is not constant over time, split follow-up into intervals and fit a piecewise-exponential model: within each interval the arm log-hazard is log((events+cc)/person-time) and each interval becomes an independent contrast-based NMA, giving interval-specific relative effects (the non-PH signal). Delegates to the netmeta-verified GLS engine; an inverse-variance pool across intervals gives a time-averaged log-HR.

Data

Interval-specific relative effects (log-HR)

Method. Piecewise-exponential (Poisson) approximation: arm log-hazard = log((events+cc)/person-time), per-interval log-HR = difference (Var ≈ 1/(events+cc) summed over the two arms). Each interval is an independent contrast-based NMA solved by shared/nma-multiarm-v1.js (verified vs netmeta ≤1e-6); interval effects are pooled by inverse variance. Interval rows use an independent-interval approximation (within-arm temporal correlation not modeled). Engine shared/nma-survival-nph.js, verified vs netmeta::netmeta() per interval. Offline. Ported from advanced-nma-pooling.
